## What we built

- `scripts/step1_leaderboard.py`: top 2,000 wallets by all-time PnL
- `scripts/step2_positions.py`: CLOSED, REDEEMABLE, and REDEEMABLE_LOST positions per wallet. Resumable.
- `scripts/step3_wallet_stats.py`: per-wallet stats, filter flags, and an edge z-score ranking
- `tests/test_offline.py`: runs the whole pipeline on fake data. Passed. In the fake data, wallets with planted skill rose to the top.

## After the --redo-hyperactive run (~93 wallets fetched)
- 77 wallets analysed, 29 pass every filter. 18 are hyperactive.
- First two wallets with z ≥ 3: **ndb1** (2,340 markets, ROI 4%, z 5.69) and **ilovecircle** (1,345 markets, ROI 3%, z 3.10).
- Median bet size varies hugely: ndb1 ~$730, ilovecircle ~$940, DrPufferfish ~$18k, hot2trot ~$67k, tazcot ~$90k. ndb1 and ilovecircle bet at a scale much closer to ours, which helps copyability.
- Rough keyword categories: all top candidates are mostly sports (54–100%).
- Pattern: high z comes with thin ROI (2–4%) over thousands of markets, while high ROI (tazcot 65%, hot2trot 31%) comes from few markets with weaker z. A 2–4% edge can easily be erased by entering a few minutes late or paying a worse price.
- Tried to tell "held to resolution" from "sold early" using positions data. **It can't be done:** positions redeemed after a market settles look the same as positions sold early (current_size = 0). This needs trade-level data (`/v2/trades` or `/v2/activity`).

## Picker vs trader (Justin's idea: use the exit price)
- Derived each position's average exit price from entry price + realized PnL ÷ shares. $1 or $0 = held to resolution; in between = sold early.
- It changes the rankings: ndb1's edge z falls from 5.69 to 1.75 on held bets (49% of profit came from early sales). DrPufferfish falls from 2.70 to 0.46; his held bets lose money and all his profit is in-play trading. S-Works rises from 2.30 to **3.11** held-only. ilovecircle: 2.56 held-only, 9% ROI on held bets.
- Added to step 3: `pct_held`, `edge_z_held`, `roi_held`, `early_sale_pnl_share`, and `flag_trader`. Ranking now uses `edge_z_held`.
- Current picker shortlist: S-Works (z 3.11, ROI 3%, ~$4.3k bets, mostly favorites at ~81c), ilovecircle (z 2.56, ROI 9%, ~$940 bets), 0x5966… (z 2.43, ROI 26%), hot2trot (z 1.93, ROI 30%), Allezpapa (z 1.82, ROI 22%).

## Can traders be copied too?
- Early sales are held for hours to days, not seconds: median ~23h for ndb1 and ilovecircle, ~10h for gmpm, ~27h for DrPufferfish. A few minutes of copy lag is not the problem.
- The problem is the size of the gain: ndb1, ilovecircle and gmpm typically make only 0–1¢ per share on early sales. Copying pays a worse price twice (on the buy and on the sell), which likely erases that.
- **DrPufferfish is the exception:** early sales win 80% of the time, median +3.4¢, average +16¢. His held bets lose money. He's a candidate to copy on both buys and sells.
- Decision: "trader" is now a `style` label in step 3, not a filter. The copy-lag backtest decides copyability for both styles. Step 3 prints pickers and traders separately.

## Copy-lag backtest built (steps 4 and 5)
- Constraint: Polymarket keeps 1-minute price history ~7 days and 5-minute ~60 days, so the lag replay covers the last ~55 days. Price sensitivity covers the full 18 months from positions data.
- step4 downloads trades (`/v2/trades`, taker_only=false) and 5-minute price history (`/v2/prices-history`) per token, cached in the `trades` and `prices` tables.
- step5 replays copies at lags of 0, 5, 30 and 60 minutes, plus a 1¢-per-side cost scenario. Missed copies (market already settled) count as 0%. It also shows a "big bets only" view and the price-sensitivity table.
- Tests: hand-checked cases (tests/test_copy_lag.py) and an end-to-end fake-API run (tests/test_step45_offline.py). All pass.

## Price-sensitivity results (18 months, held bets, same stake on every bet)
- High-volume thin-edge wallets don't survive paying 2¢ more: S-Works 6.0% → −1.2%, 0x7fB7… 3.2% → −1.1%, Kch-Temp 4.2% → −0.1%, DrPufferfish 4.1% → −2.8%.
- Robust: hot2trot (22.8% → 17.7% at +2¢), muchobliged, gfjoigfsjoigsjoi, tazcot, Inaccuratestake, fengdubiying (11.0% → 9.5%), Allezpapa (14.0% → 8.3%), 0x5966… (12.5% → 8.2%). Caveat: few bets each (21–187).
- **Big finding: equal-stake vs their-size returns differ.** ilovecircle makes +8.3% on his own sizes but −3.1% with equal stakes. ndb1: 4.9% vs 0.5%. geniusMC: 9.8% vs 0.3%. Their edge is in *how much* they bet.
- **Conviction filter:** copying only their top-25% / top-10% bets by size, at +2¢: ilovecircle −5.9% → +5.0% / +8.6%; ndb1 → +5.3% (top 10%); geniusMC → +10.3% (top 10%); Allezpapa 8.3% → 19.8% / 35.4%; fengdubiying → 14.4% / 35.2%. Small samples, and trying several cutoffs risks overfitting, so this needs out-of-sample confirmation.
